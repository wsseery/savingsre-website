SavingsRE is the real estate brand of William Spencer Seery Jr, PA, working with United Realty Group ("SavingsRE", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through savingsre.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email bill@savingsre.com or call +1 (561) 220-0402.

What we collect

What you send us. When you fill out an inquiry form or survey, we collect what you enter, such as your name, email, phone number and the details of what you are looking for, and the page you sent it from.

How you use the site. Like most websites, we use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics, which use cookies and similar technology to tell us which pages are visited, how visitors arrive (for example from an email or an ad) and which links are clicked. This information does not include your name unless you give it to us.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, send you properties and market updates you asked for, schedule showings and calls, provide real estate services, keep records we are required to keep, and understand which parts of the site are useful. Inquiries are stored in our customer-relationship software (Agile CRM), which may send you an automatic confirmation by email and text.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number on a form, you agree that we may contact you by call or text, including automated texts, about your real estate inquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time, or HELP for help. Agreeing to texts is never a condition of buying, selling or renting. We do not sell or share your phone number or text-messaging consent with third parties for their own marketing.

When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to provide the service you asked for, for example with a listing agent, landlord, lender, title company or insurance agency you ask us to connect you with; with service providers that run our website, email, texting and CRM on our behalf; and when the law requires it.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to other websites, including FlexMLS for listing details. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle information.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails with the link in any email, stop texts by replying STOP, and ask us to update or delete your contact information by emailing bill@savingsre.com. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Security and changes

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you share, but no website or transmission is completely secure. This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children. If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date.

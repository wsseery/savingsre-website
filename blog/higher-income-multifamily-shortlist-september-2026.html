Higher-Income Multifamily Shortlist — 18 SE-Florida Deals, Ranked by Cap Rate

We screened the on-market small-multifamily field across Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade for the one thing that actually pays you: income. Eighteen made the cut — four units and up, each penciling a 6%+ cap on reported net operating income, ranked highest first.

This is an income-first list, not a price list. Every property here reports its actual income and expenses, so the cap rates shown are computed from reported net operating income — not a broker's stabilized teaser. We set a floor of a 6% going-in cap and four units, then ranked the field highest yield first. Seventeen are on-market listings that publish their financials; one is a coastal Miami Beach eight-unit. The result is a tight, comparable set an investor can actually underwrite against, from a $676,000 entry point up to $4,000,000.

The read: the yield is a step outside the core. The highest going-in caps — 9.42% in Lake Park, 9.17% and 8.72% in Riviera Beach, 8.53% in West Palm Beach — sit in the workforce-rent corridors, not the compressed coastal metros where small-multifamily is trading sub-6%. That is the whole trade right now. If you want a double-digit-adjacent cap on real income, you buy the corridor and the management, not the ZIP code. Entry is genuinely accessible: the lowest-priced deal on the list is a $676,000 four-unit still penciling 9.17%, and several strong caps sit under $1M.

For buyers who want beachside exposure, the list carries one coastal option — a Miami Beach eight-unit at a 7.00% cap, the rare on-market coastal deal that still underwrites to a 7. Everything here is broker-reported and unverified, so treat the shortlist as a starting point: confirm income, expenses, and unit count against the offering memorandum and a trailing-12 before you write. That's exactly the work we do with clients on the ones that survive the first cut.

The SavingsRE take
  • Rank by cap on reported NOI, not asking price — a $676K four-unit at 9.17% beats a prettier building at 5%.
  • The double-digit-adjacent yield is in Lake Park, Riviera Beach and WPB corridors — core coastal small-MF is sub-6%; buy the corridor and the management.
  • Want coastal? There's one on-market Miami Beach 8-unit still at a 7.0% cap — most aren't.

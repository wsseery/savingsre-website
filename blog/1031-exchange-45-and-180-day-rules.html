1031 Exchange 45 and 180 Day Rules: The Two Clocks Every Exchange Runs On

The 1031 exchange 45 and 180 day rules set two deadlines that start together. Here is what each one requires, and why it can help to start your replacement search before you sell.

The two numbers: 45 and 180

If you are planning a 1031 exchange, the 1031 exchange 45 and 180 day rules are the two numbers to know first. Per the IRS instructions for Form 8824, replacement property must be identified within 45 days after the property you are giving up is transferred. It must be received within 180 days after the transfer, or by your tax return due date (including extensions), whichever is earlier.

The two 1031 exchange clocks
ClockWhat it requires
45 daysIdentify the replacement property, counted from the transfer of the property you give up
180 daysReceive the replacement property, or by your return due date (including extensions) if that is earlier

Source: IRS, Instructions for Form 8824 (2025), irs.gov/instructions/i8824, read October 20, 2026.

Both clocks start together

The 180-day clock does not begin after the 45-day clock ends. Both start when the property you are giving up is transferred, so the days you use to identify a replacement are also days off the window to close. The “whichever is earlier” wording matters too: if your tax return due date, including extensions, comes before the end of the 180 days, that earlier date is your deadline for receiving the replacement property.

What qualifies as like-kind

Since 2018, like-kind exchanges apply only to real property held for business or investment use. Whether a specific property or situation qualifies is a question for your tax adviser.

Spend the 45 days choosing, not searching

If you are selling, it can help to start looking at replacement properties early, so the 45 days are spent choosing rather than searching. Knowing your target property type, area and price range before the transfer lets you compare real options inside the identification window instead of starting from a blank page.

The SavingsRE take
  • Two clocks, 45 days to identify and 180 days to receive, start together at the transfer.
  • The deadline to receive can be earlier than 180 days if your return due date, including extensions, comes first.
  • Starting the replacement search before you sell can leave the identification window for choosing.
  • Your tax adviser and qualified intermediary decide the details of your exchange.

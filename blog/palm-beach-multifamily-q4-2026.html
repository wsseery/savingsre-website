Palm Beach County Multifamily, Q4 2026: Thin Average Yields, Better Odds in Smaller Deals

The county’s average multifamily cap rate sits near 5% while mortgage rates sit above 7%. Here’s what that gap means heading into 2027 — and where the numbers still work.

The numbers

Multifamily snapshot: Palm Beach vs. Broward

Palm Beach / Boca Raton4.98%7.3%$2,546

Source: CBRE / Yardi Matrix, late 2025 – Q1 2026, as compiled on SavingsRE Market Intel (updated October 17, 2026).

Palm Beach multifamily trades at the lowest average cap rate of the markets we track — 4.98%, against 6.27% in Broward — with vacancy at 7.3% and the highest average asking rent, $2,546. The same sources report net absorption outpacing new deliveries in both Broward and Palm Beach: renters are taking up new units faster than they’re being delivered.

Debt is the constraint

The 30-year fixed averaged 7.37% on October 16 (daily lender average), and Freddie Mac’s weekly survey printed 7.40% for the week of October 15. Commercial multifamily loans price on their own terms, but the direction is the same: when a building yields about 5% and money costs more than 7%, leverage subtracts from the return instead of adding to it.

That shifts the conversation from “what’s the market cap rate” to “what does this building actually earn, and how is it financed.” More equity, seller financing, assumable debt or real rent upside are what make a Palm Beach deal work at today’s rates.

What’s holding up demand

For-sale supply is tight. Palm Beach County single-family homes for sale were down 19.6% year over year in September, while the median single-family price rose 6.7% to $656,000 (MIAMI REALTORS + RWorld, September 2026). Fewer homes to buy at higher prices can keep households renting longer.

West Palm Beach keeps adding anchors. Vanderbilt University has raised $400 million toward a $520 million goal for its West Palm Beach graduate campus (The Real Deal, citing the Palm Beach Post, October 12, 2026), and the city’s office pipeline is about 3.3 million square feet, with roughly 700,000 under construction (CBRE / Yardi Matrix).

Insurance is easing at the residential level. Southeast Florida county homeowners rate filings of −11% to −14% were approved and took effect July 1, 2026 (Florida OIR). Commercial multifamily policies price separately, and coastal, older and condo properties may see less relief — quote the specific building before you underwrite it.

Where the numbers still work

Averages hide the spread. Smaller 2–20 unit buildings and value-add properties price one deal at a time, and that’s where above-average yields show up. Every Tuesday we rank active South Florida listings by the numbers — cap rate, price per unit, NOI — so you start with the short list. The latest lists went live October 20, 2026, including Delray Beach multifamily, multifamily up to $3M, and high-yield multifamily.

The SavingsRE take
  • Don’t pay an average-cap price for a building you can’t improve. Underwrite actual rents and actual expenses, not the county average.
  • Plan the capital stack first. At 7%+ money, the deals that work carry more equity, seller financing or a clear rent upside.
  • Selling to buy? Start the replacement search before your sale closes — 1031 timelines are welcome here.

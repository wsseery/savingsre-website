Florida Retail Space for Lease: What Storefront Tenants Are Seeing in Q3 2026

If you are searching for Florida retail space for lease or a storefront in Palm Beach, Broward or Miami-Dade, here is what the Q3 2026 broker reports say about vacancy and asking rents, and what changed in Florida’s tax on commercial rent.

Storefront searches tend to start with a map and a price. Before you compare one listing’s rent with another’s, it helps to know how tight the market is, how the rent is quoted, and which costs sit on top of it. This note pulls those three things together from public sources.

How tight is South Florida retail?

Lee & Associates’ Q3 2026 South Florida retail overview puts the regional vacancy rate at 3.50% and the average NNN asking rate at $36.87 per square foot. Cushman & Wakefield’s Q3 2026 MarketBeats report Miami-Dade retail vacancy at 3.1% and Palm Beach County at 3.7%. Low single-digit vacancy means fewer open storefronts to choose from at any one time.

Different firms measure different inventories, so their numbers do not line up exactly. Cushman & Wakefield, for example, reports a higher Miami-Dade average asking rent of $50.27 per square foot. Use any one report to compare areas with each other, not to price a single storefront.

Read the rent the way the listing quotes it

Most South Florida retail is quoted on a net basis. An NNN (triple net) rent covers the space itself; the tenant also pays a share of property taxes, property insurance and common-area maintenance (CAM). A gross or modified gross rent folds some or all of those costs into one number. Two storefronts with the same asking rent can cost very different amounts each month once those charges are added, so ask for the current estimate of the extra charges with every listing.

Florida no longer taxes most commercial rent

Effective October 1, 2025, Florida repealed the state sales tax on commercial rent under section 212.031, Florida Statutes, according to the Florida Department of Revenue. No state sales tax or local surtax applies to rent for rental periods that begin on or after that date. Rent for periods through September 2025 is still taxable, even if it is paid later. Short-term (transient) rentals, parking and storage fall under different rules.

Source: Florida Department of Revenue, Tax Information Publication 25A01-04, “Sales Tax on Commercial Rentals Repealed Effective October 1, 2025.” Read November 24, 2026.

The SavingsRE take
  • Vacancy across the three counties is in the low single digits in the Q3 2026 reports, so the choice of open storefronts at any moment is limited.
  • Compare rents on the same basis: an NNN number and a gross number are not the same thing.
  • Leases for periods starting on or after October 1, 2025 no longer carry Florida’s state sales tax on commercial rent.

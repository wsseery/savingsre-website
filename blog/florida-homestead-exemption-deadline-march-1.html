Florida Homestead Exemption Deadline March 1: Qualify on January 1, File by March 1

The first-time exemption is not applied for you. Here is how the two dates work.

The Florida homestead exemption deadline is March 1, and it catches new owners off guard because the exemption is not applied for you the first time. Under the statute (Fla. Stat. 196.011), you qualify based on how things stand on January 1, and you file with your county property appraiser on or before March 1.

Qualify on January 1, file by March 1

Two dates do the work. January 1 is the date the home must be your permanent residence. March 1 is the date your application is generally due. So if you closed on a home in 2026 and it is your permanent residence on January 1, 2027, your application is generally due by March 1, 2027.

Miss it, and the statute treats that year’s exemption as waived, with limited exceptions. The practical lesson for a new owner: put March 1 on the calendar now, not after the first tax bill arrives.

Two things new owners often don’t expect

  • Your assessment may not match the seller’s. After a sale, Florida generally reassesses the home at just value as of the next January 1, so the seller’s tax bill is not a reliable guide to yours.
  • Renewal can be automatic once you qualify. In Palm Beach County, once you qualify, the property appraiser renews the exemption for you each year as long as you continue to qualify.

What to bring when you file

The Palm Beach County Property Appraiser offers e-filing and lists what to bring, including Social Security numbers for owners and spouses. Counties run their own offices and websites, so check your own county’s site before you file.

Where the rules come from

The filing rules are in Fla. Stat. 196.011, and the exemption itself is grounded in the Florida Constitution, Art. VII s.4. If you want the exact text, read the statute and the constitution rather than a summary, including this one. We have intentionally left out exemption dollar amounts here; confirm current figures with your county property appraiser.

The SavingsRE take
  • You generally qualify based on how things stand on January 1, and you file on or before March 1.
  • The first-time exemption is not applied for you, and a missed deadline is generally treated as a waiver for that year, with limited exceptions.
  • Expect a reassessment after a sale, and check your own county’s site for how to file.

Source: Fla. Stat. 196.011; Fla. Const. Art. VII s.4; Palm Beach County Property Appraiser – Homestead Exemption, read Oct 20, 2026.

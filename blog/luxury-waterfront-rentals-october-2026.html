Luxury Waterfront Rentals in Palm Beach County: the Q4 Window

Heading into winter, South Florida's best ocean and Intracoastal rentals lease first. Here's what's on the market now across Delray Beach, Highland Beach and Boca Raton — and how working with a tenant's agent changes the search.

Why Q4 is the window

Waterfront rental demand in Palm Beach County builds through the fourth quarter as seasonal residents plan winter move-ins. The best ocean-view and Intracoastal units — the ones with the right building, floor and exposure — tend to lease early, well before the January peak. If a winter lease is on your radar, starting the search in October rather than December is the difference between choosing from inventory and chasing what's left.

What's on the market now

Our Luxury Waterfront Rentals page tracks roughly 25 active waterfront rentals in Palm Beach County in the $5,000–$10,000 a month range, concentrated in three coastal submarkets: Delray Beach, Highland Beach and Boca Raton. The mix spans ocean-view and Intracoastal condos and boutique buildings along the A1A corridor.

A few examples from this week's set: a two-bedroom condo at 45 Ocean in Highland Beach around $5,200 a month; a condo at Hamilton House in Delray Beach near $6,000; and, at the top of the band, a two-bedroom unit at Sea Ranch Club in Boca Raton around $10,000. The full set, refreshed weekly from FlexMLS, lives on the page with the live listing detail.

The SavingsRE take
  • Lease the building and the exposure, not just the unit — on the water, floor, view and HOA rules matter as much as the rent.
  • Start early. The strongest Q4 inventory is gone before the winter season fully arrives.
  • Use a tenant's agent. We represent you through the search, the application and the lease. In most rentals the landlord pays the agent fee — we'll confirm before you sign.

We represent you as the tenant from the first search through lease signing: pulling the live MLS set to your criteria, arranging tours, and negotiating terms. Not renting? The same page connects to our sale listings and investor board for buyers and owners across residential and commercial.

South Florida Market Intel, October 2026: Rates Near 7.6%, Inventory Down 20%+, Yields to 15%

Financing got more expensive again, the supply of homes for sale is still shrinking, and prices are holding. Here is where Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach stand, and where the yield is for investors this week.

Rates: the cost of money moved up again

The daily lender average for a 30-year fixed conforming loan reached 7.63% on October 7, and the 30-year jumbo average was 7.71%. Freddie Mac's weekly survey had the 30-year at 7.28% for the week of October 1; the next weekly print lands October 8. For most South Florida counties the 2026 jumbo threshold is $832,750, so a large share of luxury purchases is priced off the jumbo number, not the conforming one.

Closed single-family sales were roughly flat to slightly down year over year (Miami-Dade -3.1%, Broward +0.1%, Palm Beach -2.0%). The story is supply: with 19% to 24% fewer homes on the market than a year ago, prices are holding even as rates rise. The next county release is due October 16.

For context, institutional multifamily cap rates run about 6.27% in Fort Lauderdale/Broward, 5.79% in Miami-Dade and 4.98% in Palm Beach/Boca, and Broward multifamily sales volume reached $1.28B, up about 20% year over year. A cap well above those benchmarks usually means a seller-stated number, a value-add property or a smaller market, so treat the list as a place to start underwriting, not a finished answer.

Insurance remains part of every hold-cost calculation. Approved homeowners rate filings in the southeast Florida counties took effect July 1 at -11% to -14%, and Citizens' policy count is down about 81% from its 2023 peak, though coastal, older and condo properties may see less relief.

The SavingsRE take
  • Buyers: price your payment off the jumbo rate if you are above $832,750, and get pre-approved before the October 8 rate print.
  • Sellers: inventory is down about a fifth from a year ago. Less competition is still your best leverage.
  • Investors: the double-digit caps are outside the core coastal metros. Verify income, expenses and unit count against the offering memorandum before you write.

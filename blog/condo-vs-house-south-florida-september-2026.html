Condo vs House in South Florida: September 2026 Medians Side by Side

Condo vs house in South Florida is mostly a question of price tier. Here are the September median sale prices for both property types in four counties, with how sales and inventory moved.

The medians, side by side

If you are weighing a condo vs house in South Florida, the gap between the two median prices is the first thing to see. The single-family median was $692,000 in Miami-Dade County, $641,500 in Broward, $656,000 in Palm Beach and $600,000 in Martin. The townhouse/condo median in the same counties was $417,090, $235,000, $310,000 and $237,500.

What sales and inventory did

The single-family market is the one with the year-over-year trend data. Closed single-family sales ran below a year ago in all four counties, and the number of homes for sale fell further.

In short, single-family closed sales were 2.7% to 7.1% below a year ago, with 16.6% to 19.6% fewer homes for sale. For context on the prior month, see our October 2026 Market Intel note.

How to read the two columns

These are county-wide medians, so they describe the middle of each market, not any one neighborhood or building. A median is also not the price of a specific home: waterfront, newer construction and amenity-rich buildings can sit well above or below it. Use the table to frame a search, then compare actual sales in the exact area, building or street you are considering.

Property type also changes the cost of owning, not just the purchase price. Condo owners should look at association fees and reserves, and house owners at maintenance and insurance. Ask for those numbers early, because they belong in any monthly-cost comparison.

The SavingsRE take
  • Buyers: decide on lifestyle and monthly cost first, then use the medians to set a realistic price range for each property type.
  • Sellers: with fewer single-family homes on the market than a year ago, accurate pricing against recent comparable sales still matters most.
  • Both: medians are a starting point. Verify details for the specific property and area.

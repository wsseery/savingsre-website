$193M Freedom Park Permit Leads a Development Pipeline That’s Still Building

The week’s largest construction permit was a $193M Miami Freedom Park filing. Add a $180M West Palm office tower and the supply story is far from over.

South Florida’s largest new construction permit of the week was a $193 million filing for Miami Freedom Park near Miami International Airport — a million-plus-square-foot retail, dining, office and hotel complex with 750 hotel rooms and a 58-acre public park. Pair that with a reported $180 million West Palm Beach office tower filing, and the region’s development pipeline is still very much in motion.

Permits are a leading indicator that too many buyers ignore. Major entitlements reshape the neighborhoods around them — they pull in jobs, infrastructure, and foot traffic that lift nearby residential and small-commercial values years before the ribbon is cut. The investors who do best aren’t the ones buying inside the mega-project; they’re the ones who bought the block next door before the permit hit the news.

That’s the lens we bring to our Market Intel feed: track the filings, map the path of capital, and position ahead of it. A $193 million permit isn’t just a headline — it’s a signal about where the next value corridor is forming.

The SavingsRE take
  • Big permits are leading indicators of neighborhood value — watch them, don’t wait for delivery.
  • The edge is buying adjacent to the mega-project, not inside it.
  • Track entitlements as a pipeline, not one-off news.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a full-service and a net office lease?

A full-service (gross) rent includes most operating costs such as taxes, insurance, maintenance and often utilities and janitorial. A net rent adds some or all of those on top. Comparing spaces fairly means converting every quote to the same basis.

How long are typical office lease terms?

Terms of three to five years are common for small and mid-size suites, with longer terms usually earning larger concessions. Short-term and executive-suite options exist for flexibility.

Who pays my tenant-representation broker?

In most office leases the landlord pays the leasing commissions, including the tenant's broker. Bill confirms the arrangement on each space before you commit.

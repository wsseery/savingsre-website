About This Search

This search tracks office property for sale from $750,000 across Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade whose listings mention medical use: medical office condominiums, clinic buildings and multi-tenant medical buildings.

Medical office has held up better than general office, because patients need in-person care and practices invest heavily in their space. For physicians and dentists, owning the building can also turn rent into equity. Bill helps owner-users and investors compare locations, build-outs and income.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should my practice buy its own office?

Owning gives control over the space and turns rent into equity, and many practices hold the property in a separate entity that leases to the practice. Compare it with a long-term lease, and involve your accountant.

What is a medical office condo?

It is an individually owned unit within a medical building, with shared common areas managed by an association. It lets smaller practices own without buying a whole building.

What makes medical office different from regular office?

Plumbing in exam rooms, higher parking needs, ADA requirements, zoning for medical use and proximity to hospitals and referral sources all affect value and usability.

About This Search

This search tracks residential income property with three or more units priced under $1 million across Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade: triplexes, fourplexes and small apartment buildings that suit first-time and growing investors.

Properties with up to four units can often be financed with residential loans, including owner-occupied options, which makes this one of the most accessible ways to start investing. Bill helps buyers check rents, expenses, insurance and condition so the numbers hold up after closing.

Each week the search is re-run, and this page shows the count, what changed, and a link to every active listing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I live in one unit and rent the others?

Yes. Owner-occupied properties with up to four units can qualify for residential financing, sometimes with a lower down payment than an investment loan. A lender confirms the options.

What expenses should I budget for?

Property taxes, insurance (including wind and flood where required), repairs and reserves, utilities you pay, and vacancy. Insurance is a major line item in South Florida, so get a quote early.

How do I estimate rental income?

Start with current leases, then compare with rents for similar nearby units. Bill can pull rental comparables before you make an offer.

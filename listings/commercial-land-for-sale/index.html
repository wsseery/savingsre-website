Frequently Asked Questions

What due diligence is needed before buying commercial land?

Zoning and land-use designation, survey and title, environmental review, utility availability, access and traffic requirements, flood zone, and the local approval process for your intended use.

What is a ground lease?

The landowner leases the land to a tenant who builds and operates on it, often for decades. It produces long-term income without selling the land.

Can I buy land subject to approvals?

Many land contracts include a feasibility period and contingencies for rezoning or site-plan approval. The terms are negotiated case by case.

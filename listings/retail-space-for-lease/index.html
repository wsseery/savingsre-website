About This Search

This search tracks retail space listed for lease across Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade counties: in-line shop space in neighborhood and grocery-anchored centers, end caps, second-generation restaurant space, and free-standing pad sites.

Retail availability in South Florida is tight, so well-located space often leases quickly. Bill works on the tenant side: shortlisting spaces that fit your trade area and frontage needs, comparing asking rents and total occupancy costs (base rent plus taxes, insurance and common-area charges), and negotiating terms such as free rent, tenant-improvement allowances and renewal options.

Each week the search is re-run and the page shows how many spaces are available, what changed, and a link to every active listing with photos and full details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who pays the broker when I lease retail space?

In most commercial leases the landlord pays the leasing commissions, including the tenant's broker. Bill confirms how the commission is handled on each space before you sign anything.

What does NNN mean on a retail lease?

On a triple-net (NNN) lease the tenant pays base rent plus a share of the property's taxes, insurance and common-area maintenance. Ask for the current NNN estimate on every space so you can compare total occupancy cost, not just base rent.

Can I lease a second-generation restaurant space?

Yes. Spaces that already have a hood, grease trap and plumbing can save significant build-out cost and time. Tell Bill your kitchen needs and he will flag the spaces that fit.

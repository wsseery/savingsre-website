Frequently Asked Questions

How are apartment buildings valued?

Mainly on net operating income: rents less operating expenses, divided by a market cap rate. Price per unit and comparable sales are useful cross-checks.

What documents should I ask for?

A current rent roll, trailing 12-month income and expense statement, leases, utility bills, insurance and tax bills, and any recent inspection or recertification reports.

Do buildings in Florida have special inspection rules?

Florida has milestone inspection and reserve requirements for certain condominium and cooperative buildings, and local recertification rules for older buildings. Confirm what applies to the building you are buying.

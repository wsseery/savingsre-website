Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy commercial property with a small down payment?

Owner-occupied commercial property can often be financed with SBA 504 or 7(a) loans, which may require less down than conventional investment loans. A lender confirms eligibility and terms.

What should I check in a commercial condo association?

Monthly dues and what they cover, reserves, any special assessments, use restrictions, signage rules and parking allocation.

How do I compare buying with my current lease?

Compare the full monthly cost of owning (mortgage, dues, taxes, insurance, maintenance) with your rent and expected increases, and factor in equity built and your down payment's opportunity cost.

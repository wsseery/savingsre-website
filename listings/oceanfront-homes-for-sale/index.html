Frequently Asked Questions

What should I ask about an oceanfront condo building?

Ask for the milestone inspection and structural reports, reserve study and funding level, any current or planned special assessments, and the building's insurance cost.

How does insurance work for oceanfront homes?

Expect separate wind and flood coverage, with cost driven by elevation, construction and mitigation features. Get quotes during your inspection period.

Can I build or renovate on an oceanfront lot?

Work seaward of Florida's Coastal Construction Control Line needs a state permit in addition to local permits, so plan for extra review time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I check before buying a luxury home in South Florida?

Flood zone and elevation, wind mitigation and roof age (both drive insurance cost), any association rules and fees, and permits for past renovations.

Do I need a buyer's agent for a luxury purchase?

A buyer's agent represents your interests in pricing, inspections and negotiation. Agent compensation is agreed in writing with you before touring; Bill explains the options up front.

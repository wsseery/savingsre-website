Frequently Asked Questions

What should I check before leasing a warehouse?

Confirm zoning allows your use, then compare clear height, loading (dock-high vs. grade-level doors), power, truck court and parking, and office build-out. Insurance and hurricane ratings also matter in South Florida.

What is flex space?

Flex space combines a front office with a warehouse or workshop area behind it. It suits contractors, light assembly, showrooms and e-commerce operators who need both.

Who pays the broker on an industrial lease?

Usually the landlord pays the leasing commissions, including the tenant's broker. Bill confirms the arrangement for each space.

About This Search

This search tracks furnished homes and condominiums offered on monthly leases of one to eleven months across Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade, the stays most winter visitors book between November and April.

The best seasonal rentals are often reserved months ahead, and condo associations may set minimum stays or limit how many times a unit can be leased each year. Bill helps visitors find furnished homes that fit their dates and checks the association rules before they commit.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I book a winter rental?

As early as you can. Popular furnished homes for January through March are often reserved well ahead of the season.

Are there extra taxes on seasonal rentals?

Florida sales tax and county tourist-development tax generally apply to rentals of six months or less, and are usually added to the rent. Confirm the total with each listing.

Do condo associations restrict seasonal rentals?

Many do, with minimum lease lengths, a cap on leases per year, or an approval process. Bill checks the rules for each property before you book.

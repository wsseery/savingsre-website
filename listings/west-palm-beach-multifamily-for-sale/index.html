Frequently Asked Questions

Why do so few listings show a cap rate?

Many small multifamily listings in the MLS report gross rent but not expenses, or nothing at all. A cap rate needs net operating income, so Bill requests the rent roll and expenses for any listing you want to compare.

What expenses should I budget for?

Property taxes, insurance (including wind and flood where required), repairs and reserves, utilities you pay, and vacancy. Insurance is a major line item in South Florida, so get a quote early.

Can I finance a fourplex with a residential loan?

Properties with up to four units can often use residential financing, including owner-occupied options. A lender confirms what applies to you.

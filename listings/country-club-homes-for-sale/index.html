Frequently Asked Questions

What is a mandatory membership community?

In some communities every homeowner must join the club and pay its initiation and dues. Others make membership optional. Confirm which applies before you buy.

What costs should I compare between clubs?

Initiation or equity fee, annual dues, food minimums, capital assessments, and the homeowner association fee, alongside the home price.

Can I tour the club before buying?

Yes. Most clubs offer tours or trial visits for serious buyers. Bill can arrange them with your home showings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are short-term rentals allowed everywhere in South Florida?

No. Many cities require registration, inspections and local contacts, some set minimum stays, and associations may ban them. Always verify the city ordinance and any association rules for the specific property.

What extra costs come with short-term rentals?

Furnishing, cleaning, platform fees, management, utilities, and Florida and county tourist-development taxes on stays of six months or less.

How should I estimate income?

Use data on comparable nearby rentals across a full year, including the slower summer season, and compare it with a long-term rental scenario as a fallback.

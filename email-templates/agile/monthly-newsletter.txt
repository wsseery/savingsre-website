HOW EVERY LIST IS RANKED
Income properties are ranked by NOI cap rate - broker-reported net operating income divided by price - counted only when both expenses and NOI are actually reported. High-yield and larger lists are curated from commercial sources and screened to a published cap-rate floor, then cross-checked against reported NOI; implausible outliers are dropped. Luxury is ranked by lowest price per square foot. Short-term-rental picks use estimated gross yield from market ADR and occupancy, not host data - an estimate, and STR legality varies by city and HOA.
Public lists are generic by design - area, type, a price band and a cap or yield band, with a reference number and no exact address. Reply with a Ref and I will send the address and the full financials.

One thing worth knowing before you price. Contracts fall through more often than sellers expect — buyer diligence, insurance quotes and appraisal gaps are all live risks, and Southern markets including Florida run the highest fall-through rates in the country. A signed contract is a weaker signal than it used to be, so pricing to a realistic close beats pricing to a headline number. The current cancellation and inventory figures are on the Market Intel page.

Insurance is now part of selling

Buyers underwrite the insurance bill before they underwrite the house, and a stale or overpriced policy on your property can cost you the contract. Florida coastal pricing has been easing off its peak — approved rate filings, Citizens depopulation and softer reinsurance are all tracked on the Market Intel page — so a fresh, competitive premium figure to hand a buyer is one of the cheapest things you can do to protect a closing.

How every list is ranked

Income properties are ranked by NOI cap rate — broker-reported net operating income divided by price — and counted only when both expenses and NOI are actually reported.
High-yield and larger lists are curated from commercial sources and screened to a published cap-rate floor, then cross-checked against reported NOI. Implausible outliers are dropped.
Luxury is ranked by lowest price per square foot within the oceanfront and country-club sets.
Short-term rental picks use estimated gross yield from market ADR and occupancy, not host data — an estimate, and STR legality varies by city and HOA.

Public lists are generic by design — area, type, a price band and a cap or yield band, with a reference number and no exact address. Reply with a Ref and I will send the address and the full financials.

Insurance, on both sides of a deal
